## 1. Is the release operationally correct?

Before interpreting performance, verify that the video has:

- HD processing
- correct public/scheduled state
- intended thumbnail or active test
- working chapters, captions, links, and playlist
- no blocking restriction
- visible end screen and pinned comment when planned

Fix these issues immediately and record them separately from creative optimization.

## 2. Reach and click decision tree

### Impressions rising, CTR falling

Check whether traffic expanded from subscribers/search into Home or Suggested. A lower CTR can be normal when YouTube exposes the video to less familiar viewers. Compare watch time and views gained, not CTR alone.

### Impressions high, CTR weak

Compare by traffic source and channel baseline. When retention among clickers is healthy, test a clearer or more compelling title/thumbnail pair. Preserve the content promise.

### Impressions low, CTR healthy

Do not rush to replace packaging. Inspect topic breadth, search demand, channel size, competition, publish timing, and whether enough data exists.

### Search strong, Browse weak

The title may satisfy high-intent queries while the thumbnail lacks browse appeal. Test a browse-led visual while keeping topic clarity.

## 3. Retention decision tree

### Sharp drop in first 30 seconds

Check whether:

- the opening restates the title instead of showing proof
- the hook promises a different result
- the introduction delays the tutorial
- the edit begins slowly
- audio or visual quality is poor

Record a production lesson. A more aggressive thumbnail can worsen the mismatch.

### Dip at a section boundary

Check whether the section feels like a conclusion, reset, or unrelated detour. Improve bridge language, visual continuity, and section framing in future edits.

### Dip during B-roll or motion graphics

Check relevance, duration, text density, sound level, and whether the visual obscures rather than proves the point.

### Spike or rewatch

Identify the exact proof, demonstration, phrasing, or visual. Use it to inform future hooks, Shorts, chapters, and thumbnails.

## 4. Continuation decision tree

### Low end-screen click rate

Check whether the recommended video is the obvious next question, whether the final CTA explains why to watch it, and whether visual space exists for the element.

### Playlist starts but poor continuation

Check playlist order, topic coherence, and whether the first video hands off naturally to the next.

### Comments ask the same unresolved question

Update the pinned comment or description when a resource/correction can help. Use repeated questions as input to the next video.

## 5. Action rules

- Use native concurrent A/B tests when eligible.
- Change one manual variable at a time.
- Keep old assets and timestamps.
- Define the review window before changing anything.
- Roll back misleading or clearly worse variants.
- Prefer channel-relative evidence over generic benchmark chasing.
