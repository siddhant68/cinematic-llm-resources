# Launch playbook

## Publication check

Immediately after the visibility change:

- open the public watch page in a signed-out or viewer-like session
- confirm title, thumbnail, description opening, chapters, captions, and HD quality
- confirm end screen, cards, playlist, and disclosures
- post and pin the approved comment when comments are available
- verify links and downloadable resources

## Comment strategy

Use the pinned comment for one of four purposes:

1. offer the promised resource
2. ask a specific experience-based question
3. add an important clarification or correction
4. direct viewers to the logical next video or playlist

Respond early to substantive comments when practical. Do not manufacture comments or engagement.

## Community post

A useful launch post can:

- show a before/after frame
- ask a poll related to the video's central tradeoff
- share one surprising failure or lesson
- point to the video with a specific reason to watch

Avoid posting the title and link with no added value.

## Packaging test

Use up to three meaningfully different title and/or thumbnail variants when the native A/B feature is available. The experiment is judged by watch-time performance, so a high-click but low-satisfaction candidate should not be treated as the winner.
