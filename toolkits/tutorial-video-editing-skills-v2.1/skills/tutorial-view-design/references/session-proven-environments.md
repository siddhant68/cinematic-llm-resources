# Session-proven environment integration

- Animate static plates subtly: slow cloud/weather motion, restrained practical-light variation, distant rain or birds. The motion should not compete with speech.
- Change environments at real section boundaries and audition every loop seam at normal playback.
- Align tabletop height/inclination, horizon, perspective, subject scale, and contact point before grading.
- Match the isolated foreground separately for each environment. Do not use one global temperature shift across sunset, rainy night, morning, and mist.
- Grade foreground exposure, contrast, white balance, saturation, and gamma first; despill separately; then add low-opacity edge wrap sampled from the background.
- Preserve natural skin and shirt detail. Night generally needs lower foreground exposure/saturation; sunset and morning need restrained warmth, not orange skin.
- Inspect hair and hands in motion; avoid a wrap or feather so broad that it becomes a halo.
