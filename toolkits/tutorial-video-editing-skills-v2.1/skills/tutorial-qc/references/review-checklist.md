# Tutorial video review checklist

## Story

- Opening proves or frames the title/thumbnail promise.
- Every section has a viewer question, progression, and payoff.
- The final recap matches section order and conclusion.

## Sources

- Originals remain immutable.
- Final A-roll links to camera originals.
- Proxies are not used accidentally for delivery.
- Palmier reference movie is not used as source.

## B-roll

- Every shot has a plan ID and information job.
- User-owned proof is prioritized.
- Stock and generated assets have rights/provenance records.
- Texture supports meaning and does not reduce legibility.

## Composite

- Mask tracks hair, hands, props, and motion.
- No spill, halo, transparency, or edge chatter.
- Foreground/background light and noise match.
- Bad cutouts use the framed-PIP fallback.

## Graphics

- Section headers and memory anchors are clear.
- Text does not repeat full narration.
- Phone-size readability and safe areas pass.
- Final recap leaves end-screen space.

## Cuts

- Abruptness checked through audio, timing, and motion first.
- J/L cuts support continuity.
- Transition effects are motivated and restrained.

## Audio

- Dialogue is stable and intelligible.
- Music changes by section and stays below speech.
- SFX has a linked event.
- All music/SFX rights are recorded.

## Technical

- Correct duration, resolution, frame rate, codec, color, and audio streams.
- No accidental black, freeze, silence, or missing media.
- Captions and chapters align to final render.
