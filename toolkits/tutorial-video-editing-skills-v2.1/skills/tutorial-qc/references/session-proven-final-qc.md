# Session-proven final QC

- Run QC after every downstream finishing step, not only on the editor mezzanine.
- Count decoded frames and compare duration with the approved map. Verify raster, frame rate, pixel format, audio channels/sample rate, loudness, and true peak.
- Treat black/freeze/silence detection as triage. Intentional section cards and rhetorical pauses must be inspected and documented rather than “fixed.”
- Inspect a presenter frame from every environment plus short motion samples around hair, hands, and gesture extremes; stills can hide edge chatter.
- After repairing one defective interval in repeated generated footage, recheck every timeline occurrence.
- Verify captions around ripple splices and spell-check against the script/glossary.
- Hash the prior approved master, deliver to a new path, and compare checksums after copying or hard-linking.
