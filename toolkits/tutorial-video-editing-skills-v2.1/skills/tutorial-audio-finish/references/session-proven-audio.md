# Session-proven audio decisions

- Programme loudness cannot prove music is audible. Measure a representative ducked music stem against dialogue and listen; roughly 15-20 LU below dialogue was a useful starting range in the completed episode.
- Record source gain, sidechain threshold/ratio, attack/release, and measured post-duck level separately. A low threshold plus 5:1 ducking made a nominally present bed disappear; gentler 2.5:1 ducking restored audibility.
- Tie one restrained SFX family to verified section cards, optionally beginning just before the visual landing. Do not add effects to ordinary B-roll cuts.
- Reset music at an intentional phrase/beat for structural cues without forcing dialogue edits onto the grid.
- Preserve rhetorical silence and inspect silence detections in context.
- Measure the encoded AAC file. Keep 48 kHz delivery and adjust pre-encode true-peak headroom from the measured result.
- Carry exact music attribution into the release handoff, including edits made to timing and level.
