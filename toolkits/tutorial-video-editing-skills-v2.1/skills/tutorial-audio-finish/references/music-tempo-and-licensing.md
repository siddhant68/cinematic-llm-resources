## Tempo guidance

Speech rhythm leads. Use BPM as a search and arrangement constraint, not a mandate to cut every bar.

- Reflective explanation: slower pulse or ambient bed.
- Standard tutorial flow: moderate, unobtrusive pulse.
- Build/demo montage: higher pulse with clean edit points.
- Critical knowledge/caveat: lower or no music.
- Result reveal: short controlled lift, then settle.

## Rights record

A track is unusable until the rights manifest records its exact source, creator, license, attribution, restrictions, and local hash. Creative Commons tracks may require exact attribution. Platform-library items can change status; preserve the receipt and date checked.

## Search quality

Search by function and arrangement, not generic mood alone. Examples:

```text
minimal electronic tutorial bed no vocals sparse midrange 90-105 bpm
subtle technology pulse clean percussion no lead melody
reflective cinematic ambient tutorial underscore no vocals
```

Audition the busiest and quietest passage under actual dialogue before selecting.
