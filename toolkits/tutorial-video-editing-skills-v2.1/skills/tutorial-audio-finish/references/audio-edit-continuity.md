# Audio edit continuity

## Dialogue edits

- Keep handles around every selected take.
- Cut at natural consonant, breath, or phrase boundaries when possible.
- Match room tone before and after the edit.
- Use crossfades short enough to preserve articulation.
- Repair mouth-click or breath collisions separately from level changes.

## J-cut applications

- introduce a new section before the section image appears
- let a question lead into the proof
- cue the next tool or screen before revealing it
- bridge from B-roll back to host interpretation

## L-cut applications

- keep narration over screen instruction
- continue a claim over result footage
- maintain host presence through a reaction or comparison
- smooth front-camera to side-camera changes

## Abruptness checklist

When a cut feels abrupt, check in order:

1. dialogue waveform discontinuity
2. missing room tone
3. music or ambience changing on the same frame
4. visual motion mismatch
5. missing anticipation or follow-through
6. insufficient hold before or after the cut

Do not add a whoosh before fixing these causes.
