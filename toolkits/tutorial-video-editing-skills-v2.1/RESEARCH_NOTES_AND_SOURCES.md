## Licensed assets

Preferred starting points:

- Pexels license: https://www.pexels.com/license/
- Pixabay license summary: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
- Mixkit licenses: https://mixkit.co/license/
- YouTube Audio Library help: https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/3376882

The agent must check the exact asset and current license at download time. A platform-level summary is not proof that every asset is clear of trademarks, privacy rights, recognizable-property restrictions, or embedded third-party material.

## Policy encoded in the skills

- Use owned evidence before generic stock.
- Download only an asset whose exact license and source page have been recorded.
- Do not infer commercial permission from search-engine visibility.
- Do not overuse transitions as a proxy for retention.
- Estimate paid-generation cost before submitting.
- Preserve camera originals through every editor handoff.
