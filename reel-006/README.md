# Reel 006 — Show Yourself

Rudra Singh enters an abandoned desert village, calls out the beast that killed its people, swears an oath, and charges. The film cuts at the instant his blade meets its horns.

## Make it in order

1. Use [the hero prompt](prompts/1_hero_character_prompt.txt) in a new ChatGPT chat to make the warrior image. This step used text only.
2. Attach that hero image and use [the beast prompt](prompts/2_beast_prompt.txt) to make the creature in the same world.
3. Attach the hero image and use [the six-angle prompt](prompts/3_six_angle_sheet_prompt.txt) to make an identity sheet.
4. Attach the hero and beast images, then use [board 1's prompt](prompts/4_storyboard_1_prompt.txt).
5. Attach the hero, beast, and finished first board, then use [board 2's prompt](prompts/5_storyboard_2_prompt.txt). Board 2 starts at board 1's final state.
6. In Seedance 2.5 omni-reference, choose vertical 9:16 and attach the hero, six-angle sheet, beast, and the board for that clip. Run [clip 1's video prompt](prompts/6_video_prompt_clip1_seedance.txt) with board 1 and [clip 2's prompt](prompts/7_video_prompt_clip2_seedance.txt) with board 2.
7. Edit the clips. In this film, the last seconds of clip 1 copied a two-shield error from its storyboard, so those seconds were removed. The warmer opening of clip 2 was removed too. A deeper voice, sound design, captions, music, and the final impact were added in the edit.

The two Seedance clips were generated at 720p, one take each. This is the record of this run, not a guarantee about future generations.
