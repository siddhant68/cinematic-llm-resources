# What today taught us: Seedance 2.5, grids, expressions, timing

Evidence base: six Seedance 2.5 generations, each reviewed at 4 frames a second, plus the older clips we reviewed:
- video B original (448b68f4, overacting)
- video B redo (cf2f7ab9, restrained, one take)
- "The Wind Takes the Flowers" take 1 (a34b90ef, shot map plus grids)
- take 2 (29b200e0, one-take attempt)
- DUST WALL and the garden clips, as the good references

Each lesson is tagged: **[seen]** means it happened on screen; **[inferred]** means it's the likely cause but untested.

## 1. Grids and storyboards

1. **A multi-panel shot map is a cut list.** [seen] Take 1's six-panel board produced exactly 5 hard cuts, one at each panel boundary (3.0 / 5.4 / 8.7 / 12.0 / 15.4 s). DUST WALL did the same, and cuts suit a thriller. Use a shot map only when you *want* those cuts.
2. **A detail grid labelled "moments inside one continuous shot" adds no cuts.** [seen] The poppy grid (P) in take 1 and the new poppy grid (P2) in take 2 each covered 4–5 s with zero cuts inside.
3. **Detail grids make hand and prop actions readable.** [seen] With only the text, take 1's tuck was hidden under her hand. With P2, which showed the hand *coming away* and the poppy visible, take 2's tuck was clean. Draw the end state you want to see, not just the action.
4. **Everything in a grid panel leaks, not just what you meant it for.** [seen] Grid G was "for the wind only", yet its dark storm clouds became take 1's sky at 3 s. His amused face in G3–G4 fought the "crestfallen" text. A "use it for X only" line does not stop the leak. Every panel has to be right in *every* respect: sky, light, faces, eyelines.
5. **A reference image's eyeline gets copied.** [seen] The original video B's over-the-shoulder reference had her looking past him, and the video copied that. Before uploading any image, check where every pair of eyes points.
6. **A still of fast motion becomes frozen motion.** [seen/inferred] Still B (the bouquet frozen mid-air) plus "the camera tilts up with the flowers" produced petals parked in the sky. Don't give stills of particles in flight. Describe speed in the text: "crosses the frame in about a second, blurred, gone".
7. **The "labels are notes only" line works.** [seen] No panel text leaked in either take, including with 2×2 grids.
8. **Panel size matters.** [inferred] A 6-panel board gives each moment about 470 px. A 2×2 grid gives about twice the detail. Use the big board for the order of events, and 2×2 grids for the hard moments.
9. **Reference budget.** Seven images were accepted, and the cost doesn't change with reference count. More references mean more chances for a contradiction, so audit each one.

## 2. Expressions

1. **Face words are acted out literally, at full size.** [seen] "Pout", "narrowed eyes" and "nose scrunch" (video B) gave a duck-face and a squint. Never name mugging expressions.
2. **Restraint text alone gives control, but also flatness.** [seen] The video B redo read as "controlled, not as expressive". Restraint is a ceiling, not a performance.
3. **Big expressions are believable when an event on screen causes them.** [seen] The gust caused her surprise, and his empty hand caused her laugh. Both were big and read as real. Put the trigger one beat before the face moves (Reel 010 rule 3).
4. **Design the end face as a ChatGPT still and pass it as a reference.** [seen] Still A (her surprise) and still C (the laugh) came through almost pose for pose in both takes.
5. **A laugh needs the body and time.** [seen] "Laughs" in 1 s (take 1) barely registered. "Bursts into a laugh, leans right back on both hands, head back, shoulders shaking", with about 2.5 s plus a settle, read as a real laugh (take 2).
6. **Surprise tends to become a gaping "O".** [seen] Take 1 held the O for about 1 s. "Lips part", plus "a wide open gaping mouth" in AVOID, made it smaller in take 2.
7. **Eyelines are written by screen direction, never with "the lens".** [seen] Side-on two-shots kept their eyes on each other in every take after the redo.
8. **At most one face change per 3 s, and a held face after each change.** [seen] Eleven face beats in 13 s (video B) became mugging.
9. **Hair belongs to the expression.** [seen] A short, sculpted curl bob in the identity image stays stiff whatever the prompt says. Loose, shoulder-length hair with weight, gusts written against the clock (not "steady wind"), and rim light against a dark background all read as real.

## 3. How to estimate clip duration (why "N things in 13 s" comes out rushed)

**The mistake:** counting *actions* and giving each about a second. Every action on screen really has three parts, **cause → action → settle**, and the audience needs the settle to read it. Seedance fits whatever you list into the time you give it. Overload it and every part gets compressed, so the clip looks rushed or mugged.

**Measured minimum times** (asked vs. what played well):

| Unit | Minimum time on screen | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Establish or connect (a look, a small smile, a touch) | 3–4 s | Take 2 connect: 4 s, smooth |
| Environment event (a gust arriving, light changing) | 2–3 s of build before it hits | Take 2 gust approach: about 2 s, read fine |
| One face change (trigger → change → hold) | 3 s | Surprise, 4.5–7.9 s in take 2; DUST WALL REACT 3 s |
| A laugh or a big release | 2.5 s + 0.5 s settle | Take 2's laugh landed; take 1's 1 s laugh didn't |
| One hand or prop verb (pluck, hold up, tuck, rest) | 1–1.5 s each, + 0.5 s hold at the end | Take 2's poppy: 4 verbs in 5 s, clean |
| A shot (if you do cut) | 3.5 s or more | Take 1's shots of 2.4–3.7 s felt choppy |
| A big camera size change | Assume it becomes a cut, so it counts as a new shot | Take 2: all 3 described moves became cuts |

**The budget rule:**
- **Total = sum of the minimums × 1.2.** The extra 20% is breathing room, and the final 1–2 s should be a hold that is still moving.
- **Rule of thumb: one beat per 4 s.** A 13 s clip holds 3 beats, a 20 s clip holds 5.
- **At most one face change per 3 s.**
- **If the budget doesn't fit, cut beats. Never compress them.** Take 1 → take 2: 7 beats became 5, the reveal and the approach shot were dropped, and the result stopped feeling rushed.

**Worked example (take 2):** connect 4 + gust 3.5 + wind dies 2.5 + laugh 4.5 + poppy 5 = 19.5 s, which fits 20 s. Take 1 had squeezed 7 beats into the same 20 s.

## 4. Other lessons

1. **A true one-take needs near-constant framing.** [seen] Take 2 asked for a push-in, a pull-back and a push-in again, and Seedance cut at every one of them despite "NO cuts". The video B redo (one gentle push) stayed a single take. For one take, use one slow move in one direction.
2. **The camera must not follow particles.** [seen] Following the flowers froze them in the frame. Hold the camera and let particles cross it.
3. **Write a weather line in every time block.** [seen] Take 2's "THE AIR:" line in each block kept petals, hair and grass moving and consistent. The stuck petals disappeared.
4. **Name how objects break.** [seen] "Bursts apart" still flew away as one bunch. "Torn apart in their hands, never as one bunch; heads rip off one by one" worked.
5. **A start frame taken from an approved take** keeps the look and staging exactly. [seen] Take 2 opened identical to take 1.
6. **A cause in the world outranks a style word.** "Real speed", "gravity", "lands on the bonnet" beat "dramatic" or "cinematic".
7. **Costs** (Seedance 2.5): 480p is 3 credits per second, 720p is 7. Run the free `get_cost` check before quoting. Higgsfield's "IN THE DARK" preset pop-up appears on these prompts; decline it with `declined_preset_id`.
8. **Working the ChatGPT app:**
   - Pasting images needs full-screen control (cmd+v).
   - Clicking a generated image opens an editor tab, and typing there sends an *edit* request.
   - Use `LANG=en_US.UTF-8 pbcopy`, or the "·" separators paste as garbage.
   - Multi-line prompts go in by paste, never typed, or each line break sends the message early.
   - Three stills can generate in parallel chats.
9. **Checking a render:** use a scene-cut detector (it finds cuts to the frame), 4 fps strips per beat, and a separate check of the face and hair. Checking by eye at normal speed missed every one of today's issues.
