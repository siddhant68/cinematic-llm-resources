# The 9-line prompt blueprint (my wording)

The skeleton behind the ad, written so you can swap in your own person and product. The full filled-in prompt is in [01_video_prompt_seedance.txt](01_video_prompt_seedance.txt).

1. **LOCK THE LOOK.** Phone selfie lens, soft daylight, real skin, no beauty filter. Paste the same words into every cut. Call it a casual tip, not an advert.
2. **TAG THE PHOTOS.** `@image1` is her, `@image2` is the product. Say each one controls looks only.
3. **WHO HOLDS THE PHONE.** Her own arm. Breathing bob, a little wrist roll, never gimbal-smooth.
4. **WRITE THE CUTS.** Ask for hard jump cuts. Say never glide or morph between shots.
5. **PIN IT TO SECONDS.** Give each beat a time span, its action and its line (0-4 s, 4-8 s...). Skip the times and the model picks the pacing: a slow start, a rushed finish. This is the one that matters.
6. **ACT AT ONE-THIRD.** Small, motivated moves, eyes first. Big acting breaks the spell.
7. **LOCK THE VOICE.** Only her, only these lines, in English with an Indian accent. Fit the words to the seconds (about 2.3 words a second).
8. **THE NO-LIST.** No glossy ad light, no extra fingers, no label changes, no generated captions.
9. **TAKE THE SHINE OFF.** In the edit: a little less contrast and colour, lifted highlights, a faint shake (see [../edit/take_the_shine_off.md](../edit/take_the_shine_off.md)).

Settings used: Seedance 2.5, reference mode, 9:16, 30 s, sound on, 480p draft (90 credits).
