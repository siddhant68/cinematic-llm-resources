TITLE AND ACT
"HOLD STILL", clip C1: alone in all that green. One continuous 7-second vertical shot, camera locked off. A young woman waits beside a small red car in the middle of an enormous windy flower farm. He is late. She is cold, bored and a little dramatic about it, and she is tiny against the sky.

LOCATION
An English-style flower farm at a cold, windy noon, exactly as in Image 1. The top half of the frame is a huge bright sky crowded with big white cumulus clouds that move fast from left to right. Below: rolling green farmland with low hedgerows, round leafy trees and a small white farmhouse with a grey slate roof far off; in the foreground a wide field of pale-green grass thick with red poppies, white ox-eye daisies, blue cornflowers, purple lupins and yellow buttercups. A pale gravel farm lane with a grass strip down the middle curves through the field. The faded red car is parked on the lane, and she stands just left of it. This layout is fixed and never rearranges.

REFERENCE ASSIGNMENT
Image 1 = the opening frame: begin exactly on it (camera position, framing, her place beside the car, the light).
Image 2 = the woman: identity lock on her copper curls, her face and her yellow sundress with thin straps and white bands at the neckline and hem.
Image 3 = the car: same small, rounded, faded cherry-red two-door with round chrome headlamps and white-wall tyres, no badges, no number plate.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action cinema, vertical 9:16, natural analogue film look, fine 35 mm grain, soft pastel greens, true red and yellow. One light source in world space: a high noon sun slightly behind her from frame left. A large cloud shadow slides across the field from the left toward her, reaches her and the car at about 5 s, dimming them, and the far hills stay sunlit. Strong steady wind from the left: the grass and flowers bend in waves, her curls and her skirt stream to the right, and a few petals blow across the frame in the foreground, middle and far distance. Real physics, real speed, no slow motion. Native sound: wind over a wide field, grass hiss, a skylark far off. No music, no dialogue.

CHARACTER
She is small in frame but readable: a woman of about twenty-six with full, silky copper-auburn curls just past the jaw, in a sunflower-yellow 1950s cotton sundress with thin straps, a fitted bodice, a white band at the neckline and a wider white band at the knee-length hem, and flat grass-green slip-on slippers. Bare arms and shoulders. Same woman as Image 2 throughout.

SHOT
Extreme wide, camera locked off on a tripod at standing height, 35 mm lens feel, deep focus. The camera does not move, zoom or cut. She and the car stay small, in the lower third, for the whole shot.

PERFORMANCE (big, clear body language so it reads from far away; one change at a time)
(0.0–1.5 s) She stands beside the car, hugging her bare arms, looking down the lane to frame right, waiting. The wind streams her curls sideways.
(1.5–3.0 s) The cold gets to her: she bounces from foot to foot twice and rubs her upper arms fast with both hands, shoulders hunched.
(3.0–4.3 s) A dramatic, fed-up "ugh": she throws her head all the way back to the sky and lets both arms flop open and drop to her sides.
(4.3–5.5 s) She gives in to the cold and hugs herself again, shoulders up to her ears, and the cloud shadow reaches her and the car.
(5.5–7.0 s) She leans back against the side of the car, arms still wrapped around herself, and turns her head to look down the lane to frame right again. Hold, still small, the wind still moving everything on the last frame.

AVOID
The camera moving or zooming in; her becoming large in frame; any other person, the man, animals or vehicles; a different car; badges, logos, number plates or text; any change to her dress, hair colour or slippers; a strapless dress; slow motion; a cut; music.
