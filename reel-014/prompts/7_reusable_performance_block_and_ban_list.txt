THE PERFORMANCE BLOCK (from the clip that went in the film; the brackets are the film's specifics, swap in yours)

She shows what she feels with her body: posture, arms, where she looks, her breath. Her face stays calm and natural. There is only one change in her face in the whole shot: [the small smile she cannot stop at the end], at about a third of its full size. Her eyes move first, her head follows, then her body. Between changes she is simply still, breathing, with her hair moving.

THE BAN LIST (the same prompt's hard constraints)

Her face stays natural. No pouting or pushed-out lips, no squinting, no raised eyebrow, no eye-roll, no nose scrunch, no wide grin, no laugh, no lips moving as if talking.

THE EYELINES (the same prompt)

Write each eyeline by screen direction, for example: "she looks up and to frame right, straight into his eyes". Never use "the lens" to mean a person. Add: "Neither of them ever looks into the lens."
