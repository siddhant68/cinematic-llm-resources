TITLE AND ACT
"HOLD STILL", clip A: she pretends to be cross with him. One 13-second vertical sequence in exactly two shots with ONE cut at 6.5 s. She has waited too long on a cold, windy flower farm; he finally walks up the lane, one hand hidden behind his back. She decides to stay cross. She is very bad at it. Every change in her face is caused by what she sees.

LOCATION
The same English-style flower farm at a cold, windy noon. A pale gravel farm lane with a grass strip down the middle runs away from the faded red car toward low hedgerows, round leafy trees and a small white farmhouse far off. Verges of red poppies, white ox-eye daisies, blue cornflowers, purple lupins and yellow buttercups. Big white clouds crossing a bright sky. The car stays parked in the same place on the lane; the layout never rearranges.

REFERENCE ASSIGNMENT
Image 1 = the opening frame of shot 1: begin exactly on it (her on the bonnet, the camera, the light).
Image 2 = the woman: strict identity lock on her face, full silky copper curls, freckles, and her knee-length sunflower-yellow strapped sundress with white bands at the neckline and hem, and flat grass-green slippers.
Image 3 = her six-angle sheet: the same woman from other angles.
Image 4 = the car: the same faded cherry-red two-door with round chrome headlamps, oval grille and white-wall tyres, no badges, no plate.
Image 5 = the man: same face, dark messy curls, moustache, cracked brown leather jacket, white T-shirt, dark jeans, steel watch, one small silver hoop.
Image 6 = the framing and state for shot 2: over his right shoulder onto her, his hand holding out the wildflowers. Use it for framing only; the video never opens on it.

STYLE
Photoreal live-action cinema, vertical 9:16, natural analogue film look, fine 35 mm grain, soft pastel greens, true yellow and red. One light source in world space: a high noon sun slightly behind her from frame left, giving her curls a bright rim in both shots; soft bright fill from the clouds. Steady wind from the left: grass, flowers, her curls and her skirt all move with it; petals drift through the frame at three depths. Real physics, real speed, no slow motion. Native sound: wind in the grass, her slippers tapping the bumper, his footsteps on gravel getting closer, fabric flutter, one short breath out through her nose. No music, no dialogue, nobody speaks.

CHARACTERS
She: about twenty-six, exact likeness of Image 2, playful and expressive, never glamorous; freckles, pink cold nose and cheeks, no makeup. He: exact likeness of Image 5, clean sun-worn face, calm, amused.

SHOT 1 (0.0–6.5 s): full figure, camera low at her knee height about three metres in front of her, 50 mm lens feel, locked off. She sits on the bonnet; her whole body and the green slippers are clearly visible; he is small, far down the lane behind her, walking toward the car, his right hand hidden behind his back.
(0.0–1.5 s) Bored: both slippers swing, alternating; the fingers of her right hand drum on the bonnet; lips pushed to one side; eyes half-lidded on the sky.
(1.5–2.5 s) A curl blows across her face; she pushes her lower lip out and puffs it straight up off her forehead. It flops back. Tiny eye-roll.
(2.5–3.3 s) Her eyes catch him far down the lane. The drumming stops. One slipper freezes mid-swing. Her lips part.
(3.3–4.6 s) Caught: she sits up straight fast, smooths her skirt down over her knees with both palms, and puts her feet together.
(4.6–6.5 s) Fake cool: she crosses her arms, lifts her chin and turns her face away to frame left, pointedly not looking at him. A smile leaks out; she presses her lips flat and scrunches her nose to kill it. One slipper starts a tiny impatient bounce. Behind her he is much closer now.

CUT at 6.5 s to SHOT 2.

SHOT 2 (6.5–13.0 s): over his right shoulder, as in Image 6. The back of his head and shoulder fill the left third, very close and soft. She now stands on the lane in front of the car, facing him about a metre and a half away, waist up, sharp, arms crossed. 85 mm lens feel, eye level, camera still.
(6.5–7.7 s) Mock-stern: her right eyebrow goes up, "really?"; lips pushed into a sideways pout; eyes narrowed on his face.
(7.7–8.3 s) His right hand comes into frame from the bottom left holding out a small, loose fistful of wildflowers from the verge: red poppies, white daisies, blue cornflowers.
(8.3–9.3 s) Her eyes drop to the flowers. The raised eyebrow slowly comes down. Her lips part a little. Her arms loosen.
(9.3–10.3 s) Her eyes flick back up to his face. One corner of her mouth twitches.
(10.3–11.6 s) She sucks in her cheek to stop the smile, looks away to frame left and breathes out hard through her nose; one hand uncrosses and pretends to fix her dress strap.
(11.6–13.0 s) She is losing: a quick nose scrunch, her eyes slide back to him and the flowers, and the corner of her mouth finally lifts into a small, crooked, guilty smile. Hold on her face; the wind still moves her curls on the last frame.

AVOID
More than one cut; a third shot; the man's face being visible in shot 2; the man taking the flowers back; her taking the flowers yet; any change to her face, hair colour, dress, slippers or the car; a strapless or sleeved dress; a sudden or wide grin; an open-mouthed laugh; lips moving as if talking; tears; nodding; looking into the lens; extra people; slow motion; music; on-screen text, logos, badges or number plates.
