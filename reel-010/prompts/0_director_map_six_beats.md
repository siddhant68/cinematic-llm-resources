# Director map · DUST WALL (one 25 s generation)

Video prompt order: TITLE AND ACT → LOCATION → REFERENCE ASSIGNMENT → STYLE → STORY → CHARACTERS → SHOT STRUCTURE (+ AVOID).

| Beat | Time | Feeling | Shot | Lines that drive it | Storyboard panel / still |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ESTABLISH | 0–3 s | curious | very wide, locked off | "tiny figure", "horizon is completely clear" | 1 / establish still |
| CONNECT | 3–8 s | first unease | slow push-in, medium, eye level | eyes closed, face to the light, lopsided smile, "does not open her eyes" | 2 / her image |
| DISRUPT | 8–13 s | tense | medium-wide drift left | only the environment changes: brown light, swinging sign, dust ribbons, birds; "No storm in frame" | 3 |
| REACT | 13–16 s | afraid | tight close-up | eyes open, smile drops, held breath; "fear before we see the threat" | 4 / react still |
| REVEAL | 16–20 s | stunned | wide reverse, low behind her | the dust wall, about a kilometre high, over the road and poles | 5 / reveal still |
| ESCAPE | 20–25 s | fleeing | low fast tracking ahead of the car | door slam, headlights, "still moving on the last frame" | 6 / escape still |

Rules: show her reaction before the reveal; keep the storm out of frame until 16 s (in AVOID); never end on the reveal;
her action continues across shots and only the framing changes; the camera moves closer as the feeling rises;
the storyboard's labels are notes for the model and must never appear in the video.

Five reference images, in order: her, her angle sheet, the establishing still, the reveal still (storm + car), the storyboard.
Settings used: Seedance 2.5 omni-reference, 25 s, 9:16, 480p, sound on (75 credits). Same prompt on Wan 3.0: 20 s, 720p, 35 credits.
