# Reel 011 — One Room, Five Feelings

One young woman, one café, one sealed envelope, one charcoal V-neck. Five lighting and performance states: joy, hope, depression, anger, horror.

## Use order

1. `prompts/01_scene_master_initial.txt` is the original ChatGPT scene draft. It requested a high-neck sweater; the approved image came after the V-neck and skin edits in files 02–04. Attach your own adult character identity image rather than reusing someone else's portrait.
2. `prompts/05_overhead_comparison.txt` and `06_window_comparison.txt` edit the approved lamp-lit master with pose and expression held approximately steady. Generated outputs are not pixel-identical except for light.
3. The five original ChatGPT start frames are `images/01_joy_start.png` through `05_horror_start.png`. Their final lighting and poses were refined in ChatGPT. The original chat prompts for every intermediate start-frame edit were not all preserved, so this pack does not claim to contain those exact inputs.
4. `prompts/07_kling_five_clips.md` contains the exact five Kling 3.0 prompts used with their matching start frames: standard mode, 9:16, 4 seconds, sound off, one generation per shot. Clip frames in `images/` show the beginning, middle, and end of each result.

## What the lighting teaches

Name the physical light **source**, its **direction**, its **color temperature**, and the **shadow behavior**. Keep the character, room, wardrobe, and prop consistent across scenes. In this film the performance and pose also change, so use the near-matched comparison stills to see more clearly what the light contributes.

All images here are original ChatGPT/Kling outputs. Pinterest photographs were visual research and are not included. Sound was made in the edit.
