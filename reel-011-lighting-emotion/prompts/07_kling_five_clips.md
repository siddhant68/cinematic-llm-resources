# Reel 011 · Kling 3.0 clip prompts

Five independent 4-second, 9:16 image-to-video shots. Each uses the named ChatGPT start still, Kling 3.0 `std`, sound off. One generation per look. These are alternate emotional readings of one unopened envelope in one café. The cuts change light **and** acting; they are not a controlled demonstration of light alone.

## 01 · Joy · `01-joy-daylight.png`

One continuous four-second photorealistic shot from the start image. Same woman in her late twenties, distinctive face and nose, short wavy brown hair, natural detailed skin without freckles, same charcoal ribbed long-sleeve V-neck, same café table and sealed tan envelope. Warm broad daylight enters through the black-framed LEFT windows, high key with open soft shadows. She is seated, turns her gaze gently toward the window and lets her genuine smile grow into a brief, quiet laugh; her shoulders release and one fingertip rests lightly on the envelope. Locked camera with only a subtle slow push, no cut. Preserve the physical direction of the sunlight throughout; do not change lighting within this shot. Hold the joyful expression for the last half-second. Avoid identity drift, exaggerated grin, new people, clothing changes, freckles, cleavage emphasis, moving furniture, opening the envelope, text, and music.

## 02 · Hope · `02-hope-master.png`

One continuous four-second photorealistic shot from the start image. Same woman, sweater, table, café architecture and sealed envelope. Outside the LEFT windows is cool blue dusk; the small metal table lamp at frame RIGHT is the clear warm amber key, making a soft local pool on her face and the letter with gentle shadow falloff into the café. Seated at the table, she looks down at the envelope, breathes in, and lets a restrained, uncertain smile touch her lips as her fingers pause on its edge. Camera stays steady with an almost imperceptible push; no cut. Preserve this exact lamp-versus-window contrast throughout. Last half-second is still. Avoid identity drift, new people, big head turns, opening the envelope, changed wardrobe, freckles, text, and music.

## 03 · Depression · `03-depression-window.png`

One continuous four-second photorealistic shot from the start image. Same woman, same charcoal V-neck and same narrow café. She stands by the black-framed LEFT window holding the still-sealed tan envelope low by her side. Cool blue dusk from the window is the only meaningful light; it grazes the near cheek and hands while the café falls into deep, soft darkness. She lowers her eyes toward the envelope, exhales once, and her shoulders sink slightly; no crying or melodrama. Locked medium shot, slight inward camera drift at most, no cut. Keep the blue window light physically consistent and let the final second settle into stillness. Avoid face drift, extra people, a new room, outfit change, freckles, cheerful smile, dramatic head turns, text, and music.

## 04 · Anger · `04-anger-standing.png`

One continuous four-second photorealistic shot from the start image. Same distinctive adult woman, charcoal ribbed V-neck, original café table, lamp and sealed envelope. She is STANDING beside the oak table, leaning forward with one palm planted on it. A narrow low-angle sunset beam from the LEFT windows cuts amber across her cheek, planted hand and envelope; its far side stays dark with long crisp table shadows. The metal table lamp and ceiling lights are off. She tightens her planted hand, lifts her gaze from the envelope to the space just past camera, then holds a restrained, unmistakably angry stare with drawn brows and clenched jaw. Subtle locked camera push; no cut, no shouting, no punching. The light remains fixed for all four seconds. Hold the stare at the end. Avoid face drift, sitting down, new people, outfit or set changes, freckles, orange color filter, text, and music.

## 05 · Horror · `05-horror-overhead.png`

One continuous four-second photorealistic shot from the start image. Same distinctive adult woman, sweater, café aisle, windows, tables and sealed tan envelope in her hand. She stands alone below ONE neutral-white ceiling downlight. Hard top light casts deep eye-socket and nose shadows; the surrounding café stays blue-black and the table lamps remain off. She notices something just beyond the lens: her breath stops, eyes widen slightly, jaw slackens by a fraction, and she goes rigid without running or speaking. Locked frontal camera with a very slow push; no cut or lighting change within the shot. Let her frightening direct gaze hold for the last second. Avoid identity drift, a new person or monster, a jump scare, wardrobe changes, freckles, added lights, text, and music.

## Edit and teaching truth

Hard cuts at 4, 8, 12 and 16 seconds make the light and performance change together. No explanatory labels or narration interrupt the first play. Afterward the tutorial names the four light controls—source, direction, color temperature, shadow—and explicitly separates the acted montage from the same-expression still comparison.
